# EnergyMarketCap

EnergyMarketCap is a market intelligence platform focused on the global energy sector.

We track energy companies, assets, valuation trends, and market movements across traditional energy, power, renewables, storage, and emerging energy markets. Our goal is to make energy market data easier to follow, compare, and understand.
